BIO

Widely regarded as one of the greatest drivers in Formula One history, Schumacher's seven World Championships stood as the outright record from 2004 until Lewis Hamilton matched it in 2020. His decade at Ferrari (1996–2006) redefined the sport's approach to team-building, physical preparation, and race-craft, delivering five consecutive titles from 2000 to 2004.

After a first retirement in 2006, he returned with Mercedes from 2010 to 2012 without a race win. A skiing accident in December 2013 left him with severe injuries; the family has kept his condition private since. Held the outright records for wins (91) and podiums (155) at the time of retirement.

Insights

calculated from the 2000–2025 dataset
METRIC DEFINITIONS
reproducible from the dataset
AVERAGE FINISH
Mean finishing position across classified finishes only. DNF/DNS/DSQ excluded from numerator and denominator.
WIN RATE
Race wins ÷ race starts. A start = lights out from the grid (formation lap counts; withdrawn entries do not).
PODIUM RATE
P1–P3 finishes ÷ race starts. Sprint podiums are not counted here.
RELIABILITY
1 − (mechanical DNFs ÷ starts). Accidents and driver-caused retirements excluded from the numerator.
DRIVER CONTRIBUTION
Driver's championship points ÷ their constructor's total points in the same season.
TEAM DOMINANCE
Constructor's championship points ÷ points scored by all constructors that season. 1.00 = the only points-scorer.
GRID → FINISH DELTA
Positions gained (−) or lost (+) between the start grid and final classification. Pit-lane starts count from P20.
POINTS-PER-RACE
Career points ÷ race starts. Preferred over raw point totals when comparing across the 2003/2010/2019/2022 scoring systems.
DNF
Any classified status other than "Finished" or "+n Lap". Fastest-lap and retirements-after-flag are still counted as finishes.
Analytical observations, not opinion. Each card carries its scope. Numbers come from the results table; no interpretive labels ("greatest", "goat") are used.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
SCOPE
Coverage is 2000–2025 only. Pre-2000 records are excluded from every number in this tool. Compare against the FIA official records for full-era claims.
TIMING
Lap-by-lap timing before 2011 is partial; 1996–2010 gaps come from race classification, not lap logs.
POINTS
Points systems changed in 2003, 2010, 2019, and 2022 (sprint). Cross-era point totals are un-normalized unless the card says "points-per-race".
NAMES
Constructor identity is a judgement call across ownership changes (e.g. Renault → Alpine, Force India → Racing Point → Aston Martin). We follow Ergast's constructorId.
CACHE
Numbers reflect the latest nightly ingest; current-season data can trail the live race by up to 24h.
